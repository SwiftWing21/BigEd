# Science Pack
> Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Science_pack
> Fetched: 2026-03-27

## Overview
Science packs are items which are required by labs to perform research. Different technology types require specific science pack combinations, multiplied by a cost factor to determine total consumption.

## Base Game Science Packs

### Automation Science Pack
- **Recipe**: 1 Copper Plate + 1 Iron Gear Wheel
- **Crafting Time**: 5 seconds
- **Raw Materials per Pack**: 2 Iron Plate, 1 Copper Plate

## Production Ratios

Optimal assembler ratio to maintain balanced production (assuming equal tier machines):
- **5:6:5:12:7:7** (Automation : Logistic : Military : Chemical : Production : Utility)

At 75 items/minute per Assembling Machine 3, this maintains synchronized output.

## Module Efficiency Comparison

**Productivity Module**: Generates 1 extra product per 25 normal outputs (~4% bonus science)

**Quality Module**:
- 0.9% chance for uncommon (100% longer duration = 2× value)
- 0.09% chance for rare (200% longer = 3× value)
- 0.009% chance for epic (300% longer = 4× value)
- 0.001% chance for legendary (500% longer = 6× value)
- **Net result**: ~1.1112% bonus science

Quality provides superior space efficiency; productivity offers superior resource efficiency.
