## Tactical Mechanics

- Walls only block melee attacks; projectile attacks go over them
- Big and behemoth biters can attack 2 tiles away, bypassing single-layer walls
- Enemies attack walls only when obstructing their path to targets they're already pursuing
- Four adjacent walls in a square formation create a visual fill effect (cosmetic only)
- Gates provide passage while maintaining defensive properties

## Additional Notes

- Cannot be teleported
- Fast replaceable
- Consumed by: Gates, Military science pack
