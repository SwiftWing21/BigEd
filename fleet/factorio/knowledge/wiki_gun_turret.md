# Gun Turret
> Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Gun_turret
> Fetched: 2026-03-27

## Description
Gun turrets are defensive structures and are the first automatic defense to be unlocked. They operate without power requirements and use magazines for ammunition that must be periodically replenished.

## Recipe

| Input | Quantity |
|-------|----------|
| Iron plate | 20 |
| Copper plate | 10 |
| Iron gear wheel | 10 |
| **Output:** Gun turret | 1 (8s craft time) |

**Total raw materials:** 40 iron plate, 10 copper plate

## Stats by Quality Tier

| Stat | Normal | Uncommon | Rare | Epic | Legendary |
|------|--------|----------|------|------|-----------|
| Health | 400 | 520 | 640 | 760 | 1000 |
| Range | 18 | 19.8 | 21.6 | 23.4 | 27 |

## Core Specifications
- **Shooting speed:** 10 rounds/second
- **Dimensions:** 2×2 tiles
- **Stack size:** 50 units
- **Mining time:** 0.5 seconds
- **Rocket capacity:** 50 (1 stack)

## Compatible Ammunition
- Firearm magazine
- Piercing rounds magazine
- Uranium rounds magazine

## Key Mechanics
- Inserters can transfer magazines between turrets (max 10 per turret)
- Fire rate matches the submachine gun
- Physical projectile damage upgrades apply multiplicatively (both ammo base damage and turret damage)
- No power consumption required
- Ammunition must be manually supplied or via inserters

## Production
Crafted by: Assembling Machine 1/2/3 or manual crafting

## Required Technology
Gun turret research unlocks construction capability
