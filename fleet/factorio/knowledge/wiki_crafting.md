# Crafting
> Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Crafting
> Fetched: 2026-03-27

## Overview
The process of "crafting" is to produce an end product off of input products using a recipe. Both players and various buildings can craft materials, with smelting being a specialized crafting type.

## Manual Crafting
- Done directly in player inventory (open with E key)
- Click left for 1 item, right for 5, shift-click for maximum
- Supports chain-crafting: Materials that you don't have but you can craft will be highlighted in orange text, and will be chain-crafted
- Can cancel operations: left-click cancels 1, right-click cancels 5, shift-left-click cancels all

### Items That Cannot Be Manually Crafted
- Ores (require furnace smelting)
- Any recipe involving liquids
- Engine units (require assembling machine)

## Automated Crafting
Automated crafting is done outside of the player's inventory, allowing for multiple products to be produced at once, in synchronous harmony.

### Key Limitation
Unlike manual crafting, each step of the craft requires another assembly machine — no automatic chain-crafting in machines.

## Key Buildings

**Resource Extraction:**
- Burner/Electric Mining Drills: Extract ore
- Offshore Pump: Extracts water and fluids
- Pumpjack: Extracts crude oil

**Processing:**
- Stone/Steel/Electric Furnaces: Smelt ore
- Assembling Machines 1–3: Production (AM2+ handle fluids)
- Oil Refinery: Processes crude oil
- Chemical Plant: Fluid processing

## Basic Smelting Example
Iron plate production requires: burner mining drill on ore field → furnace → fuel (coal/logs)
