## Planet Overview
Vulcanus is a hot volcanic planet with mountainous volcanoes, rivers of lava, plains of ash, and sulfuric acid geysers. It is exclusive to the Space Age expansion and features no pollution mechanics.

## Unique Enemies: Demolishers
Demolishers are giant centipede-like boss entities with defined territorial zones outlined in bright red on the map. Each territory contains exactly one demolisher that patrols its borders and attacks player structures built within. Demolisher deaths are permanent.

## Biomes
- **Ashlands**: Plateaus with sparse vegetation, coal deposits, and flaming alien trees
- **Lava Basins**: Winding lava rivers containing tungsten ore requiring big mining drills
- **Mountains**: Volcanoes with sulfuric acid geysers and calcite deposits

## Exclusive Resources
- Tungsten ore (mineable only here)
- Lava (via offshore pumps)
- Calcite (primary source via mining)

## Crafting Recipes (Vulcanus-Only)
- Metallurgic science pack
- Foundry
- Big mining drill
- Turbo transport belt (belt, underground, splitter variants)
- Acid neutralisation

## Power Generation
- Solar panels on Vulcanus give 4× the power compared to Nauvis (400% solar)
- Acid neutralisation generates 500°C steam usable in turbines
