# Storage
> Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Storage
> Fetched: 2026-03-27

## Storage Types & Capacities

### Item Storage
- **Stacks**: Basic storage unit occupying inventory slots
- **Chests**: Primary storage containers with expandable capacity
- **Cars**: 80-slot inventory capacity
- **Cargo Wagons**: 40-slot storage capacity
- **Transport Belts**: Amount of items on a transport belt can be deceptively high
  - Belt storage: 80 items per 10 belts when using both sides
- **Splitters & Underground Belts**: Hold small quantities of items

### Machine Buffers
- Assembling machines and production equipment hold minimal items in output slots
- Machines halt operations until output items are removed

## Fluid Storage Systems

Fluids require dedicated infrastructure and cannot mix types in containers:

- **Storage Tanks**: Holds up to 25,000 fluid units (chest equivalent)
- **Fluid Wagons**: Equivalent to 2 storage tanks; rail-transportable
- **Pipes**: Transport mechanism for fluids
- **Barrels**: Hold 50 fluid units each; stackable as items

## Buffering Mechanics

Buffers maintain intermittent resource availability, critical for slow-to-produce items and ensuring continuous production/mining operations.
