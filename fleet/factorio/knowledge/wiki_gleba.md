## Planet Overview
Gleba is a moist, agricultural planet exclusive to the Space Age expansion. It features fungal hills and swampy lowlands inhabited by pentapods — spider-like enemies attracted to spores (the planet's biological pollution equivalent).

## Biomes
- **Dark Highlands**: Stone deposits found here
- **Midlands**: Orange or turquoise, elevated, no shallow water; natural barrier against pentapods
- **Marshes**: Blue biomes adjacent to deep water lakes
- **Red Marshlands**: Jellystem trees; copper/iron stromatolites abundant
- **Green Marshlands**: Yumako trees; copper/iron stromatolites abundant

Pentapods nest only on shallow water tiles, making midlands and highlands natural barriers.

## Unique Mechanics

### Spoilage System
Most biological products spoil inevitably. A timer begins at creation; the item transforms to spoilage when it reaches zero. Timescale ranges from minutes to 2 hours depending on item type. Nutrients have a 5-minute spoil time. Managing spoilage is central to Gleba factory design.
