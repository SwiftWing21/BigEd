# Asteroids
> Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Asteroids
> Fetched: 2026-03-27
> DLC: Space Age

## Overview
Asteroids are floating minerals found in space, exclusive to the Space Age expansion. They appear around stationary space platforms and during interplanetary travel at increased rates.

## Asteroid Types & Stats

| Type | Health (Standard / Promethium) | Behavior | Damage |
|------|-------------------------------|----------|--------|
| Chunk | N/A | Harmless, instantly destroyed | None |
| Small | 100 / 200 | Produces chunks when destroyed | Low |
| Medium | 400 / 800 | Splits into small asteroids | Medium |
| Big | 2,000 / 4,000 | Splits into medium asteroids | High |
| Huge | 5,000 / 10,000 | Splits into big asteroids | Severe |

## Damage Resistances

**Small / Medium:**
- Electric: 100%
- Fire: 100%
- Explosion: 50% / 30%
- Laser: 20% / 90%
- Physical: 0% / 10%

**Big:**
- Electric: 100%
- Fire: 100%
- Laser: 95%
- Explosion: 10%
- Physical: 2,000 flat + 10%

**Huge:**
- Electric: 100%
- Fire: 100%
- Laser: 99%
- Explosion: 99%
- Physical: 3,000 flat + 10%

## Resource Processing

| Asteroid Chunk Type | Primary Output | Secondary Output |
|---------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Metallic | Iron ore | Copper ore |
| Carbonic | Carbon | Sulfur |
| Oxide | Ice | Calcite |
| Promethium | Used in crafting science packs | N/A |

## Key Mechanics

- **Collection**: Asteroid collectors harvest chunks without platform damage
- **Processing**: Crushers grind chunks into raw materials
- **Research**: "Advanced asteroid processing" unlocks alternate recipes for additional yields
- **Defense**: Turrets on platform edges break asteroids into harmless chunks
- Asteroid density scales with platform velocity and current route location
- Large asteroids (Big/Huge) damage the platform on impact if not intercepted
- Chunks (the smallest form) are harmless and collected directly
