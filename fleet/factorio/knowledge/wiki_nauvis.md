## Planet Overview
Nauvis is the primary setting in Factorio's base game and the first of five visitable planets in the Space Age expansion. Described as "an Earth-like planet featuring a variety of biomes, such as deserts, lakes of liquid water, and forests."

## Biomes
- **Grasslands**: Plains with extensive tree coverage
- **Sandy Desert**: Barren terrain with scattered dead trees
- **Red Desert**: Barren reddish terrain

## Natural Resources

**Non-renewable resources (ore patches):**
- Iron ore
- Copper ore
- Coal
- Stone
- Uranium ore

**Renewable resources:**
- Water (from offshore pumps; infinite supply)
- Crude oil (from pumpjacks; regenerates to 20% minimum)
- Wood (from trees; renewable via wood processing)
- Raw fish (from water bodies)

**Uranium ore requirement**: Requires continuous sulfuric acid supply; cannot be harvested with burner mining drills.

## Exclusive Items & Mechanics

**Only found on / crafted on Nauvis:**
- Raw fish
- Uranium ore
- Fish breeding
- Wood processing (yields tree seeds)
- Biter egg production
- Captive biter spawner placement
- Biolab construction

## Pollution Mechanics
Many buildings produce pollution while active. Pollution is local to the chunk on which it is produced, but spreads to adjacent chunks over time. Pollution reaching enemy spawners triggers attacks on the player's factory.

## Space Routes
Connected to three planets at 15,000 km distance each:
- Vulcanus
- Fulgora
- Gleba
